In a metropolitan county such as Orange County, SB 4 sets a minimum density of 30 units per acre. Where the zone does not allow residential use at all, the minimum rises to 40 units per acre. These are floors, not targets: a project must reach at least that many homes per acre to use SB 4’s by-right approval. Projects may also build one story above the local height limit, which helps reach the minimum on a tight lot.
