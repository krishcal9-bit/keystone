Dear leaders of Oakfield Community Church,

We are writing on behalf of Habitat for Humanity of Orange County with an idea to talk through together, not a finished proposal. Your congregation has served Santa Ana since 1979, and the land you steward on that corner could open a door for working families who cannot buy a home in this city today.

A 2023 state law, SB 4, allows a religious institution to build affordable housing on land it owned before January 1, 2024, with ministerial approval and no discretionary or CEQA review, as long as the project meets objective standards. The law is in effect through January 1, 2036.

With your sanctuary and every existing building untouched, and keeping 60 of your 120 parking spaces, we estimate about 2.5 buildable acres on the open land and the rest of the lot. That is room for roughly 107 Habitat homes as low-rise stacked flats, where about 364 people would live. Each home is sold to a qualified lower-income family with an affordable mortgage, built in part with their own hands, and protected by a 45-year deed restriction so it stays affordable.

None of this is a decision today. If the board is interested, we would like to visit, listen to what must stay exactly as it is, count cars on your busiest Sunday, and answer every question honestly. Legal confirmation belongs to the city and your counsel.

With respect and gratitude,

Land partnerships team, Habitat for Humanity of Orange County
